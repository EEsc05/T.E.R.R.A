T.E.R.R.A — WHERE OUR DATA COMES FROM
===================================

The latest models use satellite radar data from 14 volcanoes in 10 countries.
There are two data sources:

NISAR GUNW
  Radar measurements from the NASA–ISRO NISAR satellite mission.

Sentinel-1 ARIA GUNW
  Radar measurements from ESA's Sentinel-1 satellites, processed into GUNW
  products by NASA/JPL's ARIA project.

Both are obtained through NASA's Alaska Satellite Facility (ASF) archive.
GUNW products measure changes between two satellite observations. We prepare
these measurements as maps of ground movement for the models.
