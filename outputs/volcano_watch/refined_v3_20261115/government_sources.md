# Official preparedness and monitoring resources

Checked: 2026-11-15T17:53:33Z. Coverage: 10 countries and all 15 physical volcano keys in dataset.json.

These are source links, not a live alert or shelter service. Government means a national, regional or municipal public authority; official_monitoring identifies public scientific providers that are not emergency-management departments. No evacuation centre is claimed to be open, and no route is represented as safe now.

## Verification and limits

- Source content was checked using web browsing/search. Search-index verification is explicitly marked when the source blocks automated fetching; it does not establish uninterrupted website availability.
- Colombia’s UNGRD preparedness article is an archive (2013/2014); it is labelled accordingly. SGC supplies the separate official-monitoring / alert-meaning reference. No archived medical advice or old alert state is reproduced.
- Mexico’s route portal contains plans with different publication years, including older routes. Government provenance is cross-checked by the [SSPC/CNPC announcement](https://www.gob.mx/sspc/prensa/en-el-estado-de-mexico-se-robustecen-acciones-preventivas-en-el-marco-del-plan-homologado-popocatepetl). Users must confirm current routes locally.
- Ecuador’s Alertas Ecuador pages retain historical alert declarations. The app must link to the source without presenting those declarations as today’s status.
- Ethiopia has verified national and Afar regional government preparedness/early-warning portals. No publicly accessible, volcano-specific route, shelter or monitoring feed was verified for Dabbahu or Erta Ale; use the regional-government contact source.
- Japan’s Sakurajima map is a dated city planning document; designated evacuation facilities do not imply open shelters. Asama’s municipal landing page was opened and contains hazard maps and preparedness links.
- GeoNet and IG-EPN are labelled official scientific monitoring, not government evacuation authorities. Government preparedness sources are provided separately for both countries.
- Peru’s INDECI page was verified through its official indexed text because direct fetching returned HTTP 418. Hawaiʻi County blocked direct fetching with HTTP 403; its indexed official content and the State of Hawaiʻi referral establish provenance.
- Dynamic warning pages may need JavaScript. No alert level, shelter availability, warning timestamp, evacuation instruction, or model-based emergency decision is copied into these files.
- Country-level resources are intentionally reused where a verified local resource was unavailable. Geographic coverage is resource coverage, not a claim of volcano-specific evacuation planning for every location.
