TRAINING & VALIDATION

How the comparison works

Six dated maps form each input. Targets are actual future acquisitions near the requested 30-day horizon; labels are not temporally interpolated. Histories use only acquisition pairs available by their forecast origin. Overlapping windows are purged between training and reporting partitions.

Configuration selection uses physical-volcano folds within the training data. Different sensors and viewing directions remain separate histories but share their physical-volcano group.

UNCERTAINTY

Spread is not confidence

CVAE spread shows variation among generated predictions. It is uncalibrated and should not be interpreted as a reliable confidence interval. The other models do not provide these samples.

READING THE MAP

A shared format, distinct sensors

NISAR L-band and Sentinel-1 C-band GUNW phase is converted with each product’s wavelength to millimetres of relative line-of-sight displacement. Their opposite native phase conventions are normalized to positive toward the satellite.

Native samples are aggregated onto a physical 32 × 32 grid. ARIA uses coherence ≥0.4, at least 50% valid native pixels per output cell, and at least 20% valid output cells per pair. Only its dominant coherent unwrapping component is retained.

Each pair is referenced to its retained-pixel median. Changing reference coverage can introduce offsets, and spatially uniform motion is removed. Atmospheric and tide corrections are not applied. Sensor differences remain after conversion.

INTERPRETATION

What these results establish

    The ARIA batch uses at most 60 connected acquisitions per selected stack. Catalogue searches, stack selection and quality checks limit coverage. A missing volcano or date means unavailable prepared data, not an absence of ground movement.

    No official hazard levels or eruption predictions are inferred from these maps.

    PROVENANCE

    Sources & reproducibility

    Volcano locations and country labels come from the Smithsonian Global Volcanism Program. GUNW products are distributed by ASF DAAC. Model artifacts, training protocol, per-case scores and input hashes are saved with the local training run.
